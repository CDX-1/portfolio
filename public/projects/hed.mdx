# What is Hed?

Hed (Head-Enabled Device) is an accessibility device that lets you fully control a computer through head and face movements, with optional voice commands. It's designed to help people
paralyzed from the neck down use a computer as anyone else would. Tilting the head up/down and left/right drives the mouse cursor, while tilting on the roll axis (ear-to-shoulder) triggers
left and right clicks.
